Douglas A-1 Skyraider

The A-1 Skyraider is an attack aircraft made by Douglas Aircraft company for the United States Navy. The Skyraider had seven major variants with each having its own sub variants. AD-1, AD-2, and AD-3 served only with the U.S. Navy. The AD-4 Served with the U.S. Navy, the French Air Force, the Royal Air Force, the Cambodian Air Force, The Chadian Air Force, the Gabonese Air Force, and served a non-combat role for the Swedish air force. The AD-5, AD-6, and AD-7 were the final three major variants, all of which served with the U.S. Navy, before being transfered to the U.S. Air Force for close air support missions in Vietnam, and finally to the KLVNCH (VNAF).


The AD-5 Skyraider was called the A-1E Skyraider by the time of the Vietnam war, the AD-5W was called A-1G, the AD-6 was called A-1H, and the AD-7 was called A-1J. They will use these designations on this site. The planes began painted an off white color, and were slowly repainted in the USAF SEA camouflage. Also, it is worth noting that originally the Skyraiders had single letter tail codes, and that these only went so far as their unit. Sometime in the 1960s, they changed it to two or even three letter tail codes, with the first letter (usually small) being the squadron letter, and the second large letter being the code for the individual aircraft. Aircraft were also sometimes moved between units without having time to be fully repainted, so squadron markings can sometimes be inaccurate. All this being said, it is quite difficult or even impossible sometimes to determine the actual unit of a plane at the time that the picture was taken. Any discrepancies here that I found will be noted in the caption under the image.
