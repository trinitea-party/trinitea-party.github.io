516th Fighter Squadron "Phi Hổ"


41st Tactical Wing - Nha Trang Air Base, Đà Nẵng Air Base

Originally, the 516th Fighter Squadron was called 2nd Fighter Squadron, and it was established in December 1961. In 1963, the army reorganized, and the 2nd Fighter squadron was renamed to the 516th Fighter Squadron. The squadron was nicknamed "Phi Hổ", which means "Flying Tigers". The squadron first flew T-28B and T-28C Trojan aircraft, and after May of 1964 began operating the A-1 Skyraider. In early 1969, the U.S. Air Force began giving A-37 Dragonfly planes to VNCH, and the 516th Fighter Squadron then switched over to the A-37.
